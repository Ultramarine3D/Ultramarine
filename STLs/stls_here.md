# STL for printed parts
## All STL's for printed parts on the Ultramarine 3D printer
___
## IF A PART IS LISTED HERE BUT NOT IN THE REPO, PLEASE CREATE AN ISSUE AND I WILL ADD IT TO THE REPO.
YOU CAN ALSO EXPORT THE PART DIRECTLY FROM THE .F3Z FILE AND ADD IT TO THE REPO. 

## Print Settings % Materials
These parts are ideally printed in PETG, PTCG, ABS, ASA or ideally PC for strength and heat resistance. (Glass or Carbon fiber filled versions of these filaments are also good options for strength and heat resistance.)


Nozzle: 0.4mm
Layer height: 0.2mm
Walls: 4-5 walls
Top/Bottom layers: 4-5 layers
Infill: 35-45%
Infill pattern: Gyroid, Cubic, Adaptive Cubic, or similar
